DeliverIQ Product Categories

DeliverIQ offers 71 product categories, sourced from the underlying Olist
e-commerce dataset. Categories range from everyday goods to specialty items,
including: agro industry and commerce, air conditioning, art, arts and
craftmanship, audio, auto, baby, bed bath table, books (general interest,
imported, technical), CDs/DVDs/musicals, christmas supplies, cine photo,
computers, computers accessories, consoles games, construction tools
(construction, lights, safety), cool stuff, costruction tools (garden,
tools), diapers and hygiene, drinks, dvds blu ray, electronics, fashion
(female clothing, bags accessories, childrens clothes, male clothing, shoes,
sport, underwear beach), fixed telephony, flowers, food, food drink,
furniture (bedroom, decor, living room, mattress and upholstery), garden
tools, health beauty, home appliances, home comfort, home construction,
housewares, industry commerce and business, kitchen dining laundry garden
furniture, la cuisine, luggage accessories, market place, music, musical
instruments, office furniture, party supplies, perfumery, pet shop, security
and services, signaling and security, small appliances (including home oven
and coffee), sports leisure, stationery, tablets printing image, telephony,
toys, and watches gifts.

Pricing: each category has an average product price and freight (shipping)
cost, based on real order history in the dataset. Product category has only
a small effect on delivery time itself — distance, traffic, and shipping
mode matter far more than what type of product is being shipped.
